# Scope and provenance

The MIT License covers project-authored extraction/build scripts, generic CPU support, host adaptation, tests, and documentation to the extent the contributors hold rights in them. It does not grant rights in The Robots of Dawn, its executable code, text, artwork, or other assets. This project is unofficial.

Users must independently supply the matching original disk and have the rights needed for their use. Disk bytes, intermediate memory, generated Inform source, extracted descriptions/labels, test output, and playable stories remain private local build products. No third-party compiler or interpreter is included.

This source set excludes the earlier translated text routines, reconstructed game-rule models, game-specific textual fixtures, editorial room-label tables, and historical development artifacts. Generic CPU instruction and platform I/O support remain. Game-specific addresses, disk layout descriptors, and hashes identify integration boundaries; they are not embedded game assets. This technical distinction is not a legal opinion or rights clearance.

Inform and Frotz retain their own licenses. Obtain them from their official projects.
